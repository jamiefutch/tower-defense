# Design reference

Numbers here mirror the data files. If you change a value in `src/data/` or `src/core/config.ts`, update this page too.
Source of truth: `src/core/config.ts`, `src/data/towers.ts`, `src/data/creeps.ts`, `src/data/waves.ts`, `src/data/maps.ts`.

## Rules

- **Grid:** 30 x 20 cells, 32 px each. Towers are 2 x 2.
- **Mazing:** a tower may only be placed if every spawn-to-exit route through the checkpoints stays open. Placing on a creep, a route cell, or an existing tower is refused. Selling reopens the cells.
- **Lives:** 20. Each ground or air creep that reaches the exit costs one. Zero lives ends the game.
- **Gold:** starts at 60. Kills pay the creep's bounty.
- **Waves:** 40. A wave clears when every creep is dead or has escaped. Clearing pays `5 + wave` plus interest.
- **Interest:** 5% of banked gold after each cleared wave, capped at 25.
- **Auto wave:** the next wave starts after 20 seconds. Calling it early (Space or the button) pays half the seconds left, rounded down.
- **Selling:** refunds 70% of the total invested, including upgrades.
- **Win:** clear wave 40. **Lose:** lives reach 0.
- **Speed:** 1x, 2x, or 3x. Pause is separate.

## Towers

Costs are build cost, then upgrade costs. Each tower has four levels in total (build plus three upgrades).

| Tower | Targets | Cost | Damage | Range | Shots/s | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pellet | ground | 10 / 12 / 20 / 35 | 4 / 6 / 9 / 14 | 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.75 | 1.2 / 1.3 / 1.4 / 1.6 | cheapest damage |
| Squirt | ground | 12 / 12 / 20 / 30 | 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 | 2 / 2 / 2.25 / 2.25 | 3.5 / 4 / 4.5 / 5 | fast, weak |
| Dart | ground + air | 25 / 20 / 30 / 45 | 14 / 20 / 28 / 40 | 3.5 / 3.5 / 3.75 / 4 | 0.8 / 0.9 / 1 / 1.1 | the all-rounder |
| Snap | ground | 30 / 25 / 40 / 55 | 9 / 13 / 18 / 25 | 3 / 3 / 3.25 / 3.5 | 0.9 / 1 / 1.1 / 1.2 | splash radius 1 to 1.5 |
| Swarm | air | 25 / 20 / 30 / 45 | 8 / 12 / 17 / 24 | 3.5 / 3.5 / 3.75 / 4 | 2 / 2.2 / 2.4 / 2.6 | anti-air only |
| Frost | ground | 20 / 15 / 25 / 35 | 0 | 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.75 / 3 | 1 | halves target speed for 2 / 2.5 / 3 / 3.5 s |
| Bash | ground | 35 / 25 / 40 / 55 | 6 / 9 / 13 / 18 | 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.75 / 3 | 1 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3 | every 4th shot stuns for 1 to 1.75 s |

Targeting modes: Nearest (default), Strongest, Weakest, First (furthest along the route).

## Creeps

HP shown is at wave 1. Each wave multiplies HP by `1.1^(wave-1)`.

| Creep | HP | Speed (cells/s) | Bounty | Armor | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 20 | 1.6 | 2 | 0 | |
| Fast | 14 | 3.0 | 2 | 0 | |
| Air | 25 | 2.2 | 3 | 0 | ignores the maze; flies between checkpoints in straight lines |
| Splitter | 40 | 1.2 | 4 | 0 | on death, spawns 2 Normal creeps |
| Armored | 60 | 1.0 | 5 | 3 | every hit loses 3 damage (minimum 1 gets through) |
| Immune | 45 | 1.4 | 5 | 0 | ignores slow and stun |
| Boss | 300 | 0.9 | 30 | 2 | appears every 10th wave |

Armor is subtracted from each hit, and every hit deals at least 1 damage. Against armored creeps, low-damage towers are close to useless.

## Wave composition

Generated by `buildWave(wave)` in `src/data/waves.ts`:

- **Every wave:** Normal creeps. Count `4 + floor(1.2 * wave)`, interval `max(0.45, 1 - 0.012 * wave)` seconds.
- **Every 3rd wave from 3:** Fast creeps.
- **Every 2nd wave from 4:** Air creeps.
- **Every 3rd wave from 6:** Splitters.
- **Every 4th wave from 8:** Armored creeps.
- **Every 5th wave from 12:** Immune creeps.
- **Every 10th wave:** one Boss.

Groups start staggered by 2.5 seconds, so they overlap rather than arriving as one block.

## Balance check

`npm run sim` plays a scripted strategy on each map: it buys towers by a fixed rotation, places each one where it covers the most route, and upgrades with spare gold. Current results with `HP_GROWTH = 1.1`:

| Map | Result |
|---|---|
| Meadow | lost at wave 38 |
| Switchback | lost at wave 34 |

Late waves are decided by air creeps. Anti-air coverage (Swarm and Dart) needs to be built along the flight lines between checkpoints, not just the ground route.

With `HP_GROWTH = 1.08` the same script won both maps, so growth is the main difficulty dial.
